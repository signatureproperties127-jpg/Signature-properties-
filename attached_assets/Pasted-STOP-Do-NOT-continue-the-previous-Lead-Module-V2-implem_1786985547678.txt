========================================================
1. CORE PRODUCT IDEA
========================================================

The CRM is NOT a collection of giant forms.

The CRM is the AGENT'S MEMORY.

The real workflow is:

CLIENT CALL
    ↓
Agent captures what client currently says
    ↓
Minimum information is saved
    ↓
Client is created
    ↓
Agent continues conversation
    ↓
More information is discovered
    ↓
CRM progressively updates the SAME requirement
    ↓
Requirement becomes richer over time
    ↓
Eventually it becomes ready for future Matching

The client should NEVER feel like:

"Broker is making me fill a 40-field form."

The client should feel:

"Broker understands what I need."

The agent should feel:

"CRM remembers everything I discussed with this client."

Requirement is NOT a property.

Requirement is NOT inventory.
